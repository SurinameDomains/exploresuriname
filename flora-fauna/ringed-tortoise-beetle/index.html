About this species

The Ringed Tortoise Beetle (Ischnocodia annulus) is a species of the family Chrysomelidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 2 times (GBIF), between 2022 and 2025. iNaturalist users have shared 2 observations from Suriname. Records come from Sipaliwini.
