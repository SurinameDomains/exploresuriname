About this species

The Ochre-bellied Flycatcher (Mionectes oleagineus) is a species of the family Tyrannidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 301 times (GBIF), between 1900 and 2024. iNaturalist users have shared 3 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Para and Brokopondo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
