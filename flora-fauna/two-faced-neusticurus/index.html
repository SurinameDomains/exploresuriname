About this species

The Two-faced Neusticurus (Neusticurus bicarinatus) is a species of the family Gymnophthalmidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 91 times (GBIF), between 1868 and 2024. iNaturalist users have shared 2 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini and Brokopondo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
