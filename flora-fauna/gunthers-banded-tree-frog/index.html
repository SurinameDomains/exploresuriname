About this species

The Günther's Banded Tree Frog (Boana fasciata) is a species of the family Hylidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 28 times (GBIF). iNaturalist users have shared 4 observations from Suriname. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
