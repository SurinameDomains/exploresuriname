About this species

The Underwood's Spectacled Tegu (Gymnophthalmus underwoodi) is a species of the family Gymnophthalmidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 79 times (GBIF), between 1960 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 9 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Brokopondo and Para. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
