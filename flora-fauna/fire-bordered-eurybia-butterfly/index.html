About this species

The Fire-bordered Eurybia Butterfly (Eurybia cyclopia) is a species of the genus Eurybia. It has been recorded in Suriname once (GBIF), in 2015. iNaturalist users have shared 1 observation from Suriname. Records come from Brokopondo.
