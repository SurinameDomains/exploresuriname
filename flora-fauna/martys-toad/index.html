About this species

The Marty's Toad (Rhinella martyi) is a species of the family Bufonidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 41 times (GBIF), between 1969 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 20 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini and Brokopondo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
