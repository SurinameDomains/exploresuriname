About this species

The Brown and Yellow Sphinx (Xylophanes ceratomioides) is a species of the family Sphingidae. It has been recorded in Suriname once (GBIF), in 2019. iNaturalist users have shared 1 observation from Suriname. Records come from Sipaliwini.
