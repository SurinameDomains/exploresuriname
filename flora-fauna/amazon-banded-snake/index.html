About this species

The Amazon Banded Snake (Rhinobothryum lentiginosum) is a species of the family Colubridae. It has been recorded in Suriname 12 times (GBIF), between 1926 and 2024. iNaturalist users have shared 2 observations from Suriname. Records come from Sipaliwini. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
