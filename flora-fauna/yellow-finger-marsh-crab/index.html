About this species

The Yellow finger marsh crab (Sesarma rectum) is a species of the family Sesarmidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 28 times (GBIF), between 1907 and 2023. iNaturalist users have shared 3 observations from Suriname. Records come from Commewijne.
