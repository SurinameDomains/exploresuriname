About this species

The Striped Kentropyx (Kentropyx striata) is a species of the family Teiidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 54 times (GBIF), between 1828 and 2023. iNaturalist users have shared 2 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Para and Commewijne. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
