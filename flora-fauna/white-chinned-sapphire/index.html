About this species

The White-chinned Sapphire (Chlorestes cyanus) is a species of the family Trochilidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 459 times (GBIF). iNaturalist users have shared 17 observations from Suriname. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
