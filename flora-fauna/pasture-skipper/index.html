About this species

The Pasture Skipper (Vehilius stictomenes) is a species of the family Hesperiidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 23 times (GBIF), between 1950 and 2025. iNaturalist users have shared 9 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Paramaribo and Para.
