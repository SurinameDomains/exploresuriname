About this species

The Sapphire-rumped Parrotlet (Touit purpuratus) is a species of the family Psittacidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 115 times (GBIF), between 1956 and 2024. iNaturalist users have shared 2 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Para and Brokopondo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
