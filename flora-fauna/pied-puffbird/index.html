About this species

The Pied Puffbird (Notharchus tectus) is a species of the family Bucconidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 627 times (GBIF), between 1861 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 16 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Para and Brokopondo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
