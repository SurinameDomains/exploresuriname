About this species

The Colombian bonneted bat (Eumops trumbulli) is a species of the family Molossidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 11 times (GBIF), between 1899 and 2025. Records come from Sipaliwini. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
