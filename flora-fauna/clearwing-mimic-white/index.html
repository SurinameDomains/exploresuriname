About this species

The Clearwing Mimic-White (Dismorphia theucharila) is a species of the family Pieridae. It has been recorded in Suriname 2 times (GBIF), between 2015 and 2025. iNaturalist users have shared 5 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Brokopondo and Sipaliwini.
