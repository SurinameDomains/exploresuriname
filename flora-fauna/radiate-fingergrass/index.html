About this species

The Radiate fingergrass (Chloris radiata) is a species of the family Poaceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 24 times (GBIF), between 1838 and 1968. Records come mainly from Commewijne, Saramacca and Sipaliwini. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
