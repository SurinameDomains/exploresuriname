About this species

The South American Sipo (Chironius multiventris) is a species of the family Colubridae. It has been recorded in Suriname 55 times (GBIF), between 1920 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 21 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Brokopondo, Sipaliwini and Para. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
