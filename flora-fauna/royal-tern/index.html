About this species

The Royal Tern (Thalasseus maximus) is a species of the family Laridae. It has been recorded in Suriname 354 times (GBIF), between 1946 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 3 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Commewijne, Paramaribo and Nickerie. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
