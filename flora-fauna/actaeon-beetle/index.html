About this species

The Actaeon Beetle (Megasoma actaeon) is a species of the family Scarabaeidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 36 times (GBIF), between 1960 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 35 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Brokopondo and Marowijne.
