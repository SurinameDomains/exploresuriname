About this species

The Chestnut weeper capuchin (Cebus castaneus) is a species of the family Cebidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 6 times (GBIF), between 2025 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 8 observations from Suriname. Records come from Brokopondo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
