About this species

The Oculate Dancer (Argia oculata) is a species of the family Coenagrionidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 478 times (GBIF), between 1938 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 39 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini and Brokopondo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
