About this species

The Porthole shovelnose catfish (Hemisorubim platyrhynchos) is a species of the family Pimelodidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 30 times (GBIF), between 1863 and 2018. iNaturalist users have shared 2 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini and Marowijne. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
