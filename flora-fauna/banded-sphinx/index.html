About this species

The Banded Sphinx (Eumorpha fasciatus) is a species of the family Sphingidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 10 times (GBIF), between 1951 and 2020. iNaturalist users have shared 2 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Paramaribo, Nickerie and Para.
