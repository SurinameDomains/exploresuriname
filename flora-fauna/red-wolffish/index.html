About this species

The Red Wolffish (Erythrinus erythrinus) is a species of the family Erythrinidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 113 times (GBIF), between 1838 and 2018. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Para and Brokopondo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
