About this species

The Northern Little Yellow-eared Bat (Vampyressa thyone) is a species of the family Phyllostomidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 5 times (GBIF), between 1996 and 2009. Records come from Sipaliwini. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
