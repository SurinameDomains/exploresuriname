About this species

The Redundant Skipper (Corticea corticea) is a species of the family Hesperiidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 28 times (GBIF), between 2017 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 9 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Paramaribo, Commewijne and Wanica.
