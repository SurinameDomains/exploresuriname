About this species

The Web-footed marsh rat (Holochilus brasiliensis) is a species of the family Cricetidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 20 times (GBIF), between 1890 and 2012. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini and Paramaribo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
