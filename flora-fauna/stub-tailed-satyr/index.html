About this species

The Stub-tailed Satyr (Taygetis virgilia) is a species of the family Nymphalidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 5 times (GBIF), between 2005 and 2025. iNaturalist users have shared 1 observation from Suriname. Records come mainly from Commewijne and Sipaliwini.
