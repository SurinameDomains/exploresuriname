About this species

The Avocado Mantis (Stagmatoptera supplicaria) is a species of the family Mantidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 16 times (GBIF), between 2012 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 21 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Brokopondo and Sipaliwini.
