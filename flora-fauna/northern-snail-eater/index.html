About this species

The Northern Snail-eater (Dipsas pavonina) is a species of the family Colubridae. It has been recorded in Suriname 22 times (GBIF), between 1891 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 8 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Para, Sipaliwini and Commewijne. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
