About this species

The Warty Banjo (Bunocephalus verrucosus) is a species of the family Aspredinidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 7 times (GBIF), between 1980 and 2014. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini and Nickerie. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
