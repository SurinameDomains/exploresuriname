About this species

The Matchwood (Didymopanax morototoni) is a species of the family Araliaceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 86 times (GBIF), between 1916 and 2017. iNaturalist users have shared 3 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Brokopondo and Para. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
