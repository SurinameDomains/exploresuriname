About this species

The Moustache catfish (Auchenipterus nuchalis) is a species of the family Auchenipteridae. It has been recorded in Suriname 30 times (GBIF), between 1865 and 2023. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Marowijne and Para. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
