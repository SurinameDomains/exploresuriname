About this species

The Green Oropendola (Psarocolius viridis) is a species of the family Icteridae. It has been recorded in Suriname 1,495 times (GBIF), between 1856 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 28 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Brokopondo and Para. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
