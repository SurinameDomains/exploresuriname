About this species

The Oval-leaf false-buttonweed (Spermacoce latifolia) is a species of the family Rubiaceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 75 times (GBIF), between 1837 and 2024. iNaturalist users have shared 3 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Paramaribo and Para.
