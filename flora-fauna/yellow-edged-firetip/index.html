About this species

The Yellow-edged Firetip (Pyrrhopyge amyclas) is a species of the family Hesperiidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 14 times (GBIF), between 1947 and 2024. iNaturalist users have shared 14 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Paramaribo and Nickerie.
