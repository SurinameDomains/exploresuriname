About this species

The Amazon river prawn (Macrobrachium amazonicum) is a species of the family Palaemonidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 35 times (GBIF), between 1907 and 1981. Records come from Commewijne. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
