About this species

The Black Compact Carpenter Ant (Camponotus novogranadensis) is a species of the family Formicidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 77 times (GBIF), in 2017. iNaturalist users have shared 1 observation from Suriname. Records come from Paramaribo.
