About this species

The Bushynose catfish, Bristlenose catfish (Ancistrus temminckii) is a species of the family Loricariidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 82 times (GBIF), between 1964 and 2020. iNaturalist users have shared 3 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Brokopondo and Para.
