About this species

The Red House Spider (Nesticodes rufipes) is a species of the family Theridiidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 2 times (GBIF), in 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 3 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Brokopondo and Para.
