About this species

The Chinese lovegrass (Eragrostis unioloides) is a species of the family Poaceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 3 times (GBIF), between 1982 and 2021. Records come mainly from Para and Sipaliwini. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
