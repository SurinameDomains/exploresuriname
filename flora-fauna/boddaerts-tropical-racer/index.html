About this species

The Boddaert's Tropical Racer (Mastigodryas boddaerti) is a species of the family Colubridae. It has been recorded in Suriname 100 times (GBIF), between 1845 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 15 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Para, Paramaribo and Sipaliwini. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
