About this species

The Caribbean Spiny Jewel Box (Arcinella arcinella) is a species of the family Chamidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 28 times (GBIF), between 1966 and 2014. iNaturalist users have shared 1 observation from Suriname. Records come from Commewijne.
