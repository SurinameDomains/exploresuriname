About this species

The Feather Lovegrass (Eragrostis tenella) is a species of the family Poaceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 5 times (GBIF), between 1913 and 1951. iNaturalist users have shared 1 observation from Suriname. Records come from Paramaribo.
