About this species

The Guianan Toucanet (Selenidera piperivora) is a species of the family Ramphastidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 902 times (GBIF), between 1857 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 14 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Brokopondo, Sipaliwini and Para. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
