About this species

The Texas Bow-legged Bug (Hyalymenus tarsatus) is a species of the family Alydidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 2 times (GBIF), between 1958 and 1963. iNaturalist users have shared 4 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Para and Paramaribo.
