About this species

The Bitias Hairstreak (Panthiades bitias) is a species of the family Lycaenidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 10 times (GBIF), between 2012 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 6 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Brokopondo, Para and Sipaliwini.
