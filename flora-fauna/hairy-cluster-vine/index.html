About this species

The Hairy cluster-vine (Jacquemontia tamnifolia) is a species of the family Convolvulaceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 15 times (GBIF), between 1851 and 2025. iNaturalist users have shared 1 observation from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Para and Saramacca.
