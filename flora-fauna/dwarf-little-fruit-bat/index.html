About this species

The Dwarf little fruit bat (Rhinophylla pumilio) is a species of the family Phyllostomidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 156 times (GBIF), between 1965 and 2017. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Brokopondo and Para. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
