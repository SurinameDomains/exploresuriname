About this species

The Pallid Cockroach (Phoetalia pallida) is a species of the family Blaberidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 2 times (GBIF), between 1959 and 2019. iNaturalist users have shared 1 observation from Suriname. Records come from Sipaliwini.
