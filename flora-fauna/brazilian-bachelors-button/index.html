About this species

The Brazilian bachelor's button (Centratherum punctatum) is a species of the family Asteraceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 6 times (GBIF), between 1972 and 2019. iNaturalist users have shared 1 observation from Suriname. Records come mainly from Para and Sipaliwini.
