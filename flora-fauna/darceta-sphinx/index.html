About this species

The Darceta Sphinx (Pachylia darceta) is a species of the family Sphingidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 15 times (GBIF), between 2006 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 10 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini and Brokopondo.
