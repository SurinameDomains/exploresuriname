About this species

The Drylands Tree Frog (Boana xerophylla) is a species of the family Hylidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 63 times (GBIF), between 1966 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 100 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Brokopondo, Sipaliwini and Para. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
