About this species

The Waved Woodpecker (Celeus undatus) is a species of the family Picidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 614 times (GBIF), between 1905 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 11 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Brokopondo, Sipaliwini and Para. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
