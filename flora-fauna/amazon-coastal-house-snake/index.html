About this species

The Amazon Coastal House Snake (Thamnodynastes pallidus) is a species of the family Colubridae. It has been recorded in Suriname 40 times (GBIF), between 1921 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 34 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Wanica, Commewijne and Nickerie. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
