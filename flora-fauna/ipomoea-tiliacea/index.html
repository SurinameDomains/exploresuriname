About this species

The Bejuco de puerco (Ipomoea tiliacea) is a species of the family Convolvulaceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 52 times (GBIF). iNaturalist users have shared 11 observations from Suriname. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
