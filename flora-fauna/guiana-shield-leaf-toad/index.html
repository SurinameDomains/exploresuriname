About this species

The Guiana Shield Leaf Toad (Rhinella lescurei) is a species of the family Bufonidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 18 times (GBIF), between 1967 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 13 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini and Brokopondo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
