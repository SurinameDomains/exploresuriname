About this species

The Checkered Tortoise Beetle (Aslamidium capense) is a species of the family Chrysomelidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 2 times (GBIF), between 2023 and 2024. iNaturalist users have shared 7 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Brokopondo and Sipaliwini.
