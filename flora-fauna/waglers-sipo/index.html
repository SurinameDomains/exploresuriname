About this species

The Wagler's Sipo (Chironius scurrulus) is a species of the family Colubridae. It has been recorded in Suriname 61 times (GBIF), between 1857 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 16 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini and Para. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
