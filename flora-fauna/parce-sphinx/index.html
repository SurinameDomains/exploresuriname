About this species

The Parce Sphinx (Callionima parce) is a species of the family Sphingidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 11 times (GBIF), between 2006 and 2025. iNaturalist users have shared 5 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Brokopondo and Para.
