About this species

The Thamyra Satyr (Taygetis thamyra) is a species of the family Nymphalidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 8 times (GBIF), between 2012 and 2024. iNaturalist users have shared 9 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Commewijne, Wanica and Paramaribo.
