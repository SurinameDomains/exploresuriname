About this species

The Tropical flatsedge (Cyperus surinamensis) is a species of the family Cyperaceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 61 times (GBIF), between 1827 and 2025. iNaturalist users have shared 6 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Para and Paramaribo.
