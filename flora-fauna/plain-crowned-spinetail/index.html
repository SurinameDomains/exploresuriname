About this species

The Plain-crowned Spinetail (Synallaxis gujanensis) is a species of the family Furnariidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 567 times (GBIF), between 1849 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 2 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Commewijne, Sipaliwini and Paramaribo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
