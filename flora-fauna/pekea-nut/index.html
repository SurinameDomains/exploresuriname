About this species

The Pekea-nut (Caryocar nuciferum) is a species of the family Caryocaraceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 41 times (GBIF), between 1915 and 2017. Records come mainly from Paramaribo, Para and Sipaliwini. Its global IUCN Red List status is Data Deficient.
