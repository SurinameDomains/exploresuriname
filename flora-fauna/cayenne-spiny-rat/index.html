About this species

The Cayenne Spiny Rat (Proechimys guyannensis) is a species of the family Echimyidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 293 times (GBIF), between 1892 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 2 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Brokopondo and Para. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
