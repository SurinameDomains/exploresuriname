About this species

The Chola Guitarfish (Pseudobatos percellens) is a species of the family Rhinobatidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 10 times (GBIF), between 1963 and 1981. Records come from Brokopondo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Endangered.
