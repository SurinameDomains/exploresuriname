About this species

The Lesmone Moth (Lesmone formularis) is a species of the family Erebidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 4 times (GBIF), between 2017 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 3 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Brokopondo, Paramaribo and Saramacca.
