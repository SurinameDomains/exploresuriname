About this species

The Altea (Nepsera aquatica) is a species of the family Melastomataceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 115 times (GBIF), between 1837 and 2025. iNaturalist users have shared 5 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Brokopondo and Marowijne.
