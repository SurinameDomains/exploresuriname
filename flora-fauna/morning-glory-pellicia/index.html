About this species

The Morning Glory Pellicia (Pellicia dimidiata) is a species of the family Hesperiidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 2 times (GBIF), between 2013 and 2024. iNaturalist users have shared 1 observation from Suriname. Records come mainly from Commewijne and Nickerie.
