About this species

The Tilda Yellow-shouldered Bat (Sturnira tildae) is a species of the family Phyllostomidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 173 times (GBIF), between 1979 and 2017. iNaturalist users have shared 1 observation from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Brokopondo and Para. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
