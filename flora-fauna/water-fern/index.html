About this species

The Water Fern (Ceratopteris thalictroides) is a species of the family Pteridaceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 18 times (GBIF), between 1932 and 2023. iNaturalist users have shared 1 observation from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Brokopondo and Paramaribo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
