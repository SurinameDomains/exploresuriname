About this species

The Esmeralda Longtail (Urbanus esmeraldus) is a species of the family Hesperiidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 2 times (GBIF), between 2018 and 2024. iNaturalist users have shared 1 observation from Suriname. Records come mainly from Saramacca and Sipaliwini.
