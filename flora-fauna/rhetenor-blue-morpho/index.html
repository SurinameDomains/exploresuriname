About this species

The Rhetenor blue morpho (Morpho rhetenor) is a species of the family Nymphalidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 11 times (GBIF), between 2008 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 7 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Para, Sipaliwini and Saramacca.
