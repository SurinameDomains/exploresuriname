About this species

The Haddad's Humming Frog (Chiasmocleis haddadi) is a species of the family Microhylidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 11 times (GBIF), between 2014 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 2 observations from Suriname. Records come from Sipaliwini. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
