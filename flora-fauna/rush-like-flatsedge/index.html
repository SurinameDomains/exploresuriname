About this species

The Rush-like Flatsedge (Cyperus luzulae) is a species of the family Cyperaceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 116 times (GBIF), between 1827 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 11 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Commewijne and Para. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
