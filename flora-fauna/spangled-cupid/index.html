About this species

The Spangled Cupid (Helicopis cupido) is a species of the family Riodinidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 140 times (GBIF), between 1959 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 101 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Commewijne, Wanica and Sipaliwini.
