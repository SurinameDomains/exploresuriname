About this species

The Steindachner's Apisto (Apistogramma steindachneri) is a species of the family Cichlidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 203 times (GBIF), between 1898 and 2023. iNaturalist users have shared 4 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Brokopondo and Para. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
