About this species

The Caribbean Martin (Progne dominicensis) is a species of the family Hirundinidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 9 times (GBIF), between 2015 and 2024. iNaturalist users have shared 2 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Para, Paramaribo and Nickerie. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
