About this species

The Magnificent Megadarner (Staurophlebia reticulata) is a species of the family Aeshnidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 150 times (GBIF), between 1902 and 2025. iNaturalist users have shared 6 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Commewijne and Para. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
