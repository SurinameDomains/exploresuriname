About this species

The Mangrove Root Crab (Goniopsis cruentata) is a species of the family Grapsidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 23 times (GBIF), between 1901 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 9 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Commewijne and Paramaribo.
