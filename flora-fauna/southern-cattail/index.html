About this species

The Southern cattail (Typha domingensis) is a species of the family Typhaceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 3 times (GBIF), between 1925 and 2023. iNaturalist users have shared 1 observation from Suriname. Records come from Brokopondo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
