About this species

The Aloeus Ox Beetle (Strategus aloeus) is a species of the family Scarabaeidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 9 times (GBIF), between 2017 and 2025. iNaturalist users have shared 12 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Brokopondo and Commewijne.
