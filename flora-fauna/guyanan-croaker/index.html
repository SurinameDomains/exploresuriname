About this species

The Guyanan croaker (Pachypops fourcroi) is a species of the family Sciaenidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 47 times (GBIF), between 1945 and 2018. iNaturalist users have shared 1 observation from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini and Nickerie. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
