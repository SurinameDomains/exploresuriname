About this species

The Dark-saddled Darner (Gynacantha membranalis) is a species of the family Aeshnidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 93 times (GBIF), between 1942 and 2025. iNaturalist users have shared 11 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Brokopondo and Para. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
