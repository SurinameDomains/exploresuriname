About this species

The Florestan Sphinx (Manduca florestan) is a species of the family Sphingidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 29 times (GBIF), between 2006 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 17 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Brokopondo, Sipaliwini and Marowijne.
