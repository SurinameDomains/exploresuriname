About this species

The Striped Lynx Spider (Oxyopes salticus) is a species of the family Oxyopidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 5 times (GBIF), between 1963 and 2025. iNaturalist users have shared 4 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Brokopondo, Commewijne and Sipaliwini.
