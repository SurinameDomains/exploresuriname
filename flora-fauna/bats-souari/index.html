About this species

The Bat's souari (Caryocar glabrum) is a species of the family Caryocaraceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 20 times (GBIF), between 1838 and 1982. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini and Marowijne. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
