About this species

The Pomarine Jaeger (Stercorarius pomarinus) is a species of the family Stercorariidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 125 times (GBIF), between 1971 and 2024. Records come mainly from Coronie and Commewijne. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
