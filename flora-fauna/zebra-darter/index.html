About this species

The Zebra Darter (Characidium zebra) is a species of the family Crenuchidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 90 times (GBIF), between 1957 and 2023. iNaturalist users have shared 1 observation from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Brokopondo and Para. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
