About this species

The Forest Flame Snake (Oxyrhopus petolarius) is a species of the family Colubridae. It has been recorded in Suriname 78 times (GBIF), between 1825 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 10 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Nickerie, Wanica and Commewijne. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
