About this species

The Least Bittern (Ixobrychus exilis) is a species of the family Ardeidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 34 times (GBIF), between 1900 and 2009. Records come mainly from Commewijne and Paramaribo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
