About this species

The Spear-winged Cattleheart (Parides neophilus) is a species of the family Papilionidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 9 times (GBIF), between 2014 and 2024. iNaturalist users have shared 1 observation from Suriname. Records come mainly from Commewijne and Sipaliwini. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
