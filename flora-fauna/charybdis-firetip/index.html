About this species

The Charybdis Firetip (Pyrrhopyge charybdis) is a species of the family Hesperiidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 2 times (GBIF), in 2014. iNaturalist users have shared 2 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Paramaribo and Sipaliwini.
