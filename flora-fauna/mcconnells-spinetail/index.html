About this species

The McConnell's Spinetail (Synallaxis macconnelli) is a species of the family Furnariidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 293 times (GBIF), between 1997 and 2026. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Brokopondo and Para. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
