About this species

The River Dancer (Argia insipida) is a species of the family Coenagrionidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 141 times (GBIF), between 1938 and 2025. iNaturalist users have shared 3 observations from Suriname. Records come from Sipaliwini. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
