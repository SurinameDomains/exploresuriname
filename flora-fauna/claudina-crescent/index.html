About this species

The Claudina Crescent (Tegosa claudina) is a species of the family Nymphalidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 13 times (GBIF), between 2016 and 2025. iNaturalist users have shared 9 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Brokopondo and Commewijne.
