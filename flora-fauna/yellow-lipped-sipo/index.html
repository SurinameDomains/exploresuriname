About this species

The Yellow-lipped Sipo (Chironius carinatus) is a species of the family Colubridae. It has been recorded in Suriname 133 times (GBIF), between 1828 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 29 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Commewijne, Para and Paramaribo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
