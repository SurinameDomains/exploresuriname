About this species

The Red Neusticurus (Neusticurus surinamensis) is a species of the family Gymnophthalmidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 4 times (GBIF), between 1890 and 2024. iNaturalist users have shared 2 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Brokopondo and Sipaliwini.
