About this species

The South American Silver Croaker (Plagioscion squamosissimus) is a species of the family Sciaenidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 53 times (GBIF), between 1853 and 2018. iNaturalist users have shared 1 observation from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Marowijne and Nickerie. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
