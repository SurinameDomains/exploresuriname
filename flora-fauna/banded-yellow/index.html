About this species

The Banded Yellow (Eurema elathea) is a species of the family Pieridae. It has been recorded in Suriname 11 times (GBIF), between 2018 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 13 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Para, Sipaliwini and Commewijne.
