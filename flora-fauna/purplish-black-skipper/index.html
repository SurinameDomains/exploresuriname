About this species

The Purplish-black Skipper (Nisoniades rubescens) is a species of the family Hesperiidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 5 times (GBIF), between 2018 and 2025. iNaturalist users have shared 3 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Commewijne, Saramacca and Sipaliwini.
