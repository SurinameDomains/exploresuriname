About this species

The Palmita (Xiphidium caeruleum) is a species of the family Haemodoraceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 82 times (GBIF), between 1654 and 2025. iNaturalist users have shared 10 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Brokopondo and Marowijne.
