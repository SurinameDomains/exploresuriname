About this species

The Silver-studded Leafwing (Hypna clytemnestra) is a species of the family Nymphalidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 44 times (GBIF), between 2012 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 30 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Brokopondo and Para.
