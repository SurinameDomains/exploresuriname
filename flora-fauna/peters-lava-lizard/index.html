About this species

The Peters' Lava Lizard (Tropidurus hispidus) is a species of the family Tropiduridae. It has been recorded in Suriname 98 times (GBIF), between 1944 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 58 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Para and Paramaribo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
