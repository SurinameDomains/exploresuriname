About this species

The Black-tailed Trogon (Trogon melanurus) is a species of the family Trogonidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 488 times (GBIF), between 1913 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 8 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Para and Brokopondo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
