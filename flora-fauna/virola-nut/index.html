About this species

The Virola nut (Virola sebifera) is a species of the family Myristicaceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 62 times (GBIF), between 1846 and 2017. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Para and Brokopondo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
