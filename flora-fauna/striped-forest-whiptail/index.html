About this species

The Striped Forest Whiptail (Kentropyx calcarata) is a species of the family Teiidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 317 times (GBIF), between 1903 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 103 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Brokopondo and Commewijne. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
