About this species

The Red-necked Woodpecker (Campephilus rubricollis) is a species of the family Picidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 1,239 times (GBIF), between 1914 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 26 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Brokopondo and Para. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
