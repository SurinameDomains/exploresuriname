About this species

The Callingcard vine (Entada polystachya) is a species of the family Fabaceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 51 times (GBIF), between 1837 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 3 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Paramaribo, Commewijne and Saramacca. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
