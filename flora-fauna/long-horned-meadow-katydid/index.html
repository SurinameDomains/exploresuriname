About this species

The Long-horned Meadow Katydid (Conocephalus saltator) is a species of the family Tettigoniidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 7 times (GBIF), between 1951 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 11 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Para, Brokopondo and Commewijne.
