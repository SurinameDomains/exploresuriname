About this species

The Crimson-crested Woodpecker (Campephilus melanoleucos) is a species of the family Picidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 864 times (GBIF), between 1857 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 21 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Commewijne and Para. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
