About this species

The Smooth Tegu (Tretioscincus agilis) is a species of the family Gymnophthalmidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 32 times (GBIF), between 1926 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 3 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Brokopondo and Sipaliwini. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
