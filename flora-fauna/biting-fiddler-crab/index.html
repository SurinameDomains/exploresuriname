About this species

The Biting Fiddler Crab (Minuca mordax) is a species of the family Ocypodidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 34 times (GBIF), between 1890 and 2021. iNaturalist users have shared 1 observation from Suriname. Records come mainly from Commewijne and Nickerie.
