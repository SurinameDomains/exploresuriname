About this species

The Caracas Rattlebox (Crotalaria micans) is a species of the family Fabaceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 32 times (GBIF), between 1837 and 2011. Records come mainly from Para, Brokopondo and Commewijne. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
