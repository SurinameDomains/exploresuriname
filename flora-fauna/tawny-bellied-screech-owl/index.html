About this species

The Tawny-bellied Screech-Owl (Megascops watsonii) is a species of the family Strigidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 166 times (GBIF), between 1921 and 2025. iNaturalist users have shared 4 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Brokopondo and Para. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
