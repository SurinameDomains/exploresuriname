About this species

The Bumblebee Millipede (Anadenobolus monilicornis) is a species of the family Rhinocricidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 6 times (GBIF), between 2020 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 6 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Commewijne, Wanica and Para.
