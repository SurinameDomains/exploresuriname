About this species

The Giant Ameiva (Ameiva ameiva) is a species of the family Teiidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 723 times (GBIF), between 1828 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 209 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Brokopondo and Commewijne. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
