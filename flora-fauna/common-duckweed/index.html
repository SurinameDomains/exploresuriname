About this species

The Common Duckweed (Lemna minor) is a species of the family Araceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 3 times (GBIF), between 1827 and 1974. Records come mainly from Coronie and Paramaribo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
