About this species

The Soldier Jupiaba (Jupiaba keithi) is a species of the family Characidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 49 times (GBIF), between 1964 and 2020. iNaturalist users have shared 4 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini and Brokopondo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
