About this species

The Mountain star orchid (Epidendrum ramosum) is a species of the family Orchidaceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 13 times (GBIF), between 1841 and 1998. Records come from Sipaliwini. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
