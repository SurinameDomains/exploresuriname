About this species

The Black-billed Thrush (Turdus ignobilis) is a species of the family Turdidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 216 times (GBIF), between 1920 and 2025. Records come mainly from Para, Sipaliwini and Commewijne. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
