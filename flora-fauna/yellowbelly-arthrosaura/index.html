About this species

The Yellowbelly Arthrosaura (Arthrosaura reticulata) is a species of the family Gymnophthalmidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 16 times (GBIF), between 1948 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 3 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini and Brokopondo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
