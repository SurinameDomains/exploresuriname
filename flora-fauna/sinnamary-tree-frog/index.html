About this species

The Sinnamary Tree Frog (Dendropsophus gaucheri) is a species of the family Hylidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 131 times (GBIF), between 1968 and 2014. iNaturalist users have shared 3 observations from Suriname. Records come from Sipaliwini. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
