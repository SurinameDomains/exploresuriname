About this species

The Glass tetra (Phenacogaster wayana) is a species of the family Characidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 24 times (GBIF), between 1966 and 2020. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini and Para. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
