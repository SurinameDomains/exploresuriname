About this species

The Jacky (Pimelodella cristata) is a species of the family Heptapteridae. It has been recorded in Suriname 248 times (GBIF), between 1891 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 4 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Para and Brokopondo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
