About this species

The Vine Sphinx (Eumorpha vitis) is a species of the family Sphingidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 9 times (GBIF), between 1963 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 15 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Paramaribo, Saramacca and Wanica.
