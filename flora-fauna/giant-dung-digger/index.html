About this species

The Giant Dung Digger (Coprophanaeus lancifer) is a species of the family Scarabaeidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 6 times (GBIF), between 1936 and 2015. iNaturalist users have shared 1 observation from Suriname. Records come from Sipaliwini.
