About this species

The Gray-headed Stingless Bee (Trigona williana) is a species of the family Apidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 10 times (GBIF), between 1998 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 3 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Brokopondo, Sipaliwini and Para.
