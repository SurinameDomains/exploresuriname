About this species

The Dwarf Umbrella Grass (Cyperus diffusus) is a species of the family Cyperaceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 6 times (GBIF), between 1910 and 1944. Records come from Sipaliwini. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
