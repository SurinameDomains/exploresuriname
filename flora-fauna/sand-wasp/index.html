About this species

The Sand Wasp (Stictia signata) is a species of the family Crabronidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 12 times (GBIF), between 1938 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 7 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Paramaribo and Sipaliwini.
