About this species

The Atlantic Hairback Fiddler Crab (Minuca vocator) is a species of the family Ocypodidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 23 times (GBIF), between 1908 and 2025. iNaturalist users have shared 1 observation from Suriname. Records come from Commewijne.
