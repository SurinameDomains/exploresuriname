About this species

The Kunapari (Chaetobranchus flavescens) is a species of the family Cichlidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 18 times (GBIF), between 1901 and 2018. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Para and Saramacca. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
