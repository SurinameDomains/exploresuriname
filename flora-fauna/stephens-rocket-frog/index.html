About this species

The Stephen's Rocket Frog (Anomaloglossus stepheni) is a species of the family Aromobatidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 35 times (GBIF), between 1970 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 11 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini and Brokopondo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
