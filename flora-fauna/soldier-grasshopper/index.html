About this species

The Soldier Grasshopper (Chromacris speciosa) is a species of the family Romaleidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 2 times (GBIF), between 2017 and 2018. iNaturalist users have shared 1 observation from Suriname. Records come from Sipaliwini.
