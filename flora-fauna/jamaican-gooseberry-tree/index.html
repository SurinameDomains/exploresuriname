About this species

The Jamaican gooseberry tree (Phyllanthus acuminatus) is a species of the family Phyllanthaceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 17 times (GBIF), between 1827 and 1905. Records come from Paramaribo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
