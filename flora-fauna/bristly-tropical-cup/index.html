About this species

The Bristly Tropical Cup (Cookeina tricholoma) is a species of the family Sarcoscyphaceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 25 times (GBIF), between 1954 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 22 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Brokopondo and Para.
