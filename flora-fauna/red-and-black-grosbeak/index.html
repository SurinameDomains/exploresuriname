About this species

The Red-and-black Grosbeak (Periporphyrus erythromelas) is a species of the family Cardinalidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 184 times (GBIF), between 1972 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 1 observation from Suriname. Records come mainly from Brokopondo, Sipaliwini and Para. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
