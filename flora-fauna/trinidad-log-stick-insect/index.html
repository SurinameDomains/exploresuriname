About this species

The Trinidad Log Stick Insect (Creoxylus spinosus) is a species of the family Pseudophasmatidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 6 times (GBIF), between 2023 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 8 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Brokopondo and Sipaliwini.
