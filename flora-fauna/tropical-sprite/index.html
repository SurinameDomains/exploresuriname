About this species

The Tropical Sprite (Nehalennia minuta) is a species of the family Coenagrionidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 106 times (GBIF), between 1939 and 2020. iNaturalist users have shared 1 observation from Suriname. Records come mainly from Para, Marowijne and Brokopondo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
