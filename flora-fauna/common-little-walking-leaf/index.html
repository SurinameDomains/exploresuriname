About this species

The Common Little Walking Leaf (Typophyllum mortuifolium) is a species of the family Tettigoniidae. It has been recorded in Suriname once (GBIF), in 2022. iNaturalist users have shared 2 observations from Suriname. Records come from Brokopondo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
