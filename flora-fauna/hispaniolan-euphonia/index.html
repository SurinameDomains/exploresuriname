About this species

The Hispaniolan Euphonia (Euphonia musica) is a species of the family Fringillidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 2 times (GBIF), between 1979 and 2005. Records come from Sipaliwini. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
