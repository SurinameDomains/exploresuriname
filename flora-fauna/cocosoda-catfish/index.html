About this species

The Cocosoda catfish (Pseudauchenipterus nodosus) is a species of the family Auchenipteridae. It has been recorded in Suriname 27 times (GBIF), between 1844 and 2017. iNaturalist users have shared 1 observation from Suriname. Records come mainly from Nickerie, Marowijne and Paramaribo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
