About this species

The Coral orchid (Rodriguezia lanceolata) is a species of the family Orchidaceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 34 times (GBIF), between 1823 and 2025. iNaturalist users have shared 2 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini and Brokopondo.
