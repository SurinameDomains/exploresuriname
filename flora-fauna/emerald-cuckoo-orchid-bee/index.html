About this species

The Emerald Cuckoo-Orchid bee (Exaerete smaragdina) is a species of the family Apidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 4 times (GBIF), between 1997 and 2022. iNaturalist users have shared 2 observations from Suriname. Records come from Commewijne.
