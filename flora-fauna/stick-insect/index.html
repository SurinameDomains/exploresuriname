About this species

The Stick insect (Phanocloidea muricata) is a species of the family Diapheromeridae. It has been recorded in Suriname 5 times (GBIF), between 2004 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 6 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Brokopondo and Sipaliwini.
