About this species

The Nodeweed (Synedrella nodiflora) is a species of the family Asteraceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 34 times (GBIF), between 1837 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 5 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Paramaribo and Nickerie.
