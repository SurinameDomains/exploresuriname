About this species

The Tambataru (Zanthoxylum rhoifolium) is a species of the family Rutaceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 19 times (GBIF), between 1925 and 2017. Records come from Sipaliwini. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
