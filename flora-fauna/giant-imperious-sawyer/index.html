About this species

The Giant Imperious Sawyer (Enoplocerus armillatus) is a species of the family Cerambycidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 11 times (GBIF), between 1938 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 7 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Brokopondo, Marowijne and Sipaliwini.
