About this species

The Fig Sphinx (Pachylia ficus) is a species of the family Sphingidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 8 times (GBIF), between 1955 and 2018. iNaturalist users have shared 4 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Paramaribo and Sipaliwini.
