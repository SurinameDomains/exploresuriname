About this species

The Streaked Sphinx (Protambulyx strigilis) is a species of the family Sphingidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 8 times (GBIF), between 2010 and 2025. iNaturalist users have shared 7 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Wanica and Brokopondo.
