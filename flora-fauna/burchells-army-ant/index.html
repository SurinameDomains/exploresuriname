About this species

The Burchell's Army Ant (Eciton burchellii) is a species of the family Formicidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 7 times (GBIF), between 2014 and 2025. iNaturalist users have shared 9 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Brokopondo, Commewijne and Sipaliwini.
