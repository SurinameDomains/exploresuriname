About this species

The Tricolored Big-eared Bat (Glyphonycteris sylvestris) is a species of the family Phyllostomidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 2 times (GBIF), in 2011. iNaturalist users have shared 1 observation from Suriname. Records come from Sipaliwini. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
