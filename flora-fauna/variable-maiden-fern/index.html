About this species

The Variable maiden fern (Christella hispidula) is a species of the family Thelypteridaceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 17 times (GBIF), between 1865 and 2026. iNaturalist users have shared 1 observation from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini and Brokopondo.
