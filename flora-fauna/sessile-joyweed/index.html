About this species

The Sessile Joyweed (Alternanthera sessilis) is a species of the family Amaranthaceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 46 times (GBIF), between 1854 and 1981. Records come mainly from Nickerie and Para. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
