About this species

The False meadowbeauty (Pterolepis glomerata) is a species of the family Melastomataceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 78 times (GBIF), between 1837 and 2025. iNaturalist users have shared 8 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Brokopondo and Para.
