About this species

The Queen's Wreath (Petrea volubilis) is a species of the family Verbenaceae. It has been recorded in Suriname 21 times (GBIF), between 1954 and 2025. iNaturalist users have shared 5 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini, Brokopondo and Commewijne.
