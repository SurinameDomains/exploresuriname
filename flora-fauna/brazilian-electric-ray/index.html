About this species

Narcine brasiliensis, the Brazilian electric ray, or sometimes called the lesser numbfish, is a species of electric ray in the family Narcinidae. It is found mostly in the coastal waters of the southwest Atlantic from Brazil to northern Argentina, though smaller populations are found throughout the Caribbean and the South-Eastern American Coast.
