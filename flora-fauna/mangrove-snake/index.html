About this species

The Mangrove Snake (Erythrolamprus cobella) is a species of the family Colubridae. It has been recorded in Suriname 202 times (GBIF), between 1828 and 2025. iNaturalist users have shared 7 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Commewijne, Saramacca and Paramaribo. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
