About this species

The Barred Monkey Frog (Callimedusa tomopterna) is a species of the family Phyllomedusidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 71 times (GBIF). iNaturalist users have shared 33 observations from Suriname. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
