About this species

The Sand knifefish (Rhamphichthys rostratus) is a species of the family Rhamphichthyidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 12 times (GBIF), between 1846 and 1998. Records come mainly from Marowijne and Sipaliwini. Its global IUCN Red List status is Least Concern.
