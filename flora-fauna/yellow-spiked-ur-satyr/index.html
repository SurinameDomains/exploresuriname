About this species

The Yellow-spiked Ur-Satyr (Posttaygetis penelea) is a species of the family Nymphalidae. It has been recorded in Suriname 9 times (GBIF), between 2018 and 2025. iNaturalist users have shared 5 observations from Suriname. Records come mainly from Sipaliwini and Commewijne.
