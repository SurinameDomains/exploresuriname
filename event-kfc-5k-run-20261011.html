KFC 5K Run

Sunday 11 October 2026 · in 2 days

International Mall Suriname, Paramaribo

Add to calendarOfficial websiteDirectionsSend on WhatsApp
Community event

The KFC 5K Run is more than just a race. It is a celebration of movement, community, and the energy that comes from taking on a challenge together. Whether you are an experienced runner aiming for a personal best, a recreational runner looking for a new challenge, or simply want to enjoy an active event with friends and family, everyone is welcome to be part of the experience. Taking place on October 11, 2026, at the International Mall of Suriname (IMS), the KFC 5K Run brings runners together for an exciting evening filled with energy, motivation, and a great atmosphere. The 5K distance offers the perfect opportunity to challenge yourself while enjoying the excitement of running alongside others. The run is open to participants who turn 10 years of age or older in 2026. Participants should be physically capable of completing the 5K distance independently. The race will start at 18:15h and has a cut-off time of one hour, meaning participants must cross the finish line no later than 19:15h. Come ready to run, challenge yourself, and enjoy an unforgettable 5K experience.

Good to know: Starts 18:15. Entry SRD 500. Organised by Momentum X.
