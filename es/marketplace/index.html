Preguntas

Is it free to sell on the Explore Suriname Marketplace?

Yes. Placing an ad is free. Fill in the form, sign in with Google at the end so you can edit your ad later, and it goes up. A new account's first ad gets a quick check by hand.

How do buyers contact me?

Directly. A Suriname mobile number gets a WhatsApp button with a message that already names your ad. You can also give an email address or a link. We never show the email you sign in with.

How long does an ad stay online?

Thirty days for items, sixty for property. Renew it with one tap from your ads page, and mark it sold the moment it goes.

Does Explore Suriname handle payments?

No. You deal with the seller yourself. Meet somewhere busy, check the item before you pay, and never send money in advance to someone you have not met.
