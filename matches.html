About this page

One Page, Every Start Time, Suriname Time

Broadcasters list kickoffs in European or North American time, and converting to Suriname time goes wrong just often enough to ruin an evening. This page does the conversion for every match, follows live games minute by minute and keeps the tables current.

Watching with friends? Browse where to eat. Waiting for kickoff? Try the daily Suriname quiz or the Switi Mini crossword.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time zone are the kickoff times on this page?

Everything is shown in Suriname time (UTC-3, no daylight saving). The time you see is the time the match starts on your clock in Suriname.

Are the scores live?

Yes. While a match is being played the score, the match minute and the goal scorers refresh in your browser about every minute, straight from the data provider. Open a match for its live timeline, statistics and line-ups.

Can I follow my own teams?

Yes. Open any match and tap the star next to a team, or pick one of the suggestions under My teams. Followed teams stay at the top of the page with their next match and last result, and the My teams button filters the schedule to just them. It is stored in your browser only; no account needed.

Which competitions are covered?

Suriname first: every Natio international for the men's, women's and youth sides, the full Suriname Major League season with its table and past champions, the SVB competitions below the top flight, local running events and the Concacaf Caribbean Cup. World football: the Champions League, Europa League, Conference League, Eredivisie, Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, Copa Libertadores, Copa Sudamericana, Concacaf competitions and MLS. Basketball: every NBA game. Fight nights: UFC, Glory and the big boxing cards. Plus Formula 1 and ATP/WTA tennis.

Are there league tables?

Yes, under Tables: the Suriname Major League (computed from the league's own results) with its roll of champions, plus the Eredivisie, Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, MLS and the NBA conferences. Each match also shows the table of its competition.

Where can I find Eerste Divisie, Tweede Divisie, women's or youth league fixtures?

In the SVB competitions section. The Surinaamse Voetbal Bond publishes those schedules and results as bulletins on svb.sr rather than as a live feed, so we link the newest bulletin for each competition instead of guessing at kickoff times.

Why are there no goal scorers for Suriname Major League matches?

The league's own feed currently publishes kickoff times and venues but not goal scorers or match events. As soon as sml.sr publishes them they can be shown here, like the other competitions.

When does Natio play next?

Every confirmed Natio fixture appears here automatically with a green Natio tag, and the next one gets the spotlight card at the top with a countdown in Suriname time.

Why the Eredivisie?

Suriname and Dutch football are family. Generations of Eredivisie stars have Surinamese roots, from Ruud Gullit and Frank Rijkaard to Virgil van Dijk.

Match data: ESPN, sml.sr and Concacaf. Glory, boxing and other local fixtures are added by hand once confirmed. Schedules rebuild every 15 minutes; live scores refresh in your browser.
