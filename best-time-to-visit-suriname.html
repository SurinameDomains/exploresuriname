Plan Your Trip

Best Time to Visit

Dry seasons, rainy seasons, turtles and festivals: when to come to Suriname.

The short answer. Come in the long dry season, mid-August to early December, or the short dry season, February to April. Come in April to July for turtles, and around Holi, Keti Koti, Divali or Owru Yari for the biggest celebrations.

Seasons

Two Dry Seasons, Two Rainy Seasons

Suriname is hot and humid all year: daytime highs of 29 to 32 °C and nights rarely below 22 °C. Instead of summer and winter there are four seasons of rain and sun.

SeasonWhenWhat to expect
Short dry seasonFebruary to AprilSunny spells, pleasant for city walks and hikes
Long rainy seasonMid-April to mid-AugustDaily heavy showers, wettest in May and June; rivers high, landscapes green
Long dry seasonMid-August to early DecemberHottest and driest; the best time for the interior
Short rainy seasonDecember to early FebruaryShowers return, often in the afternoon

Rain usually comes in short, heavy bursts, so even the wet months have plenty of dry hours. The country lies south of the hurricane belt.

Plan Around

Seasons for Wildlife and Festivals

  • Sea turtles: nesting from about February to August, most leatherbacks from April to July. See turtle watching.
  • Birdwatching is good all year; go at first light.
  • Interior lodges and river trips are easiest in the long dry season, when trails are dry; some rapids and sulas are only exposed when rivers are low.
  • Big celebrations: Holi Phagwa (February or March), Keti Koti on 1 July, Divali (October or November), Independence Day on 25 November and Owru Yari on 31 December.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the best time to visit Suriname?

The long dry season from mid-August to early December and the short dry season from February to April are the most comfortable. September to November is best for interior trips.

When is the rainy season in Suriname?

The long rainy season runs from about mid-April to mid-August, and a short rainy season from December to early February. Showers are usually heavy but short.

When can you see sea turtles in Suriname?

Turtles nest from about February to August, with the most leatherbacks from April to July.

Does Suriname get hurricanes?

Suriname lies south of the Atlantic hurricane belt and is very rarely hit directly.
