About Explore Suriname

South America’s best-kept secret, uncovered

What is Explore Suriname?

Explore Suriname is an independent online guide to everything Suriname has to offer: restaurants, hotels and guesthouses, nature reserves, activities, shopping, local services, live currency rates, flight schedules, weather forecasts and daily news.

Suriname is one of the most biodiverse and culturally rich countries in South America, yet it remains largely undiscovered by international travellers. Our goal is to change that by providing accurate, up-to-date information for visitors and locals alike.

The directory currently covers more than 700 businesses and attractions across Paramaribo and beyond, and is updated continuously. If you have a listing to add or a correction to suggest, please get in touch.

What we cover

✓ Restaurants & Bars: Surinamese, Indonesian, Chinese, Indian, and international cuisine across Paramaribo
✓ Hotels & Lodges: from city hotels to rainforest eco-lodges and overwater bungalows
✓ Nature & Activities: jungle treks, river tours, wildlife reserves and UNESCO sites
✓ Shopping & Services: local retailers, markets and professional services in Paramaribo
✓ Market Rates: SRD exchange rates updated three times daily (business days) from CBVS and CME
✓ Flights, Weather & News: real-time data to help you plan your stay in Suriname
