Markets in Suriname

Which market is on today, where it is and what to buy: from the Centrale Markt on the Waterkant to the Sunday markets in Kwatta and Saoenah.

The short answer. Paramaribo's main market, the Centrale Markt on the Waterkant, is open Monday to Saturday from early morning until early afternoon. On Sunday it is closed and the neighbourhood markets take over: Kwatta for farm produce, Saoenah for Javanese food, the Tourtonnelaan for second-hand finds and the Cultuurtuin for orchids. Go early: most markets are busiest before 10 in the morning.

Good to know

Tips for Visiting a Market

  • Go early. Markets start around dawn; the Centrale Markt winds down in the early afternoon and Sunday markets are mostly over by midday.
  • Bring cash in small SRD notes. Most stalls do not take cards.
  • Ask before you photograph people, especially at the Vreedzaam Market.
  • Keep your phone and wallet close in the crowds around the Centrale Markt.
  • Parking near the Centrale Markt is hard on weekdays. A taxi, or a walk from the inner city, is easier.

Once a year

Night Markets and Fairs

Night markets in Suriname are events rather than fixed weekly markets. Pasar malam night markets with Javanese food are held around Javanese Immigration Day on 9 August, and fairs and pop-up markets appear throughout the year. See the events calendar for what is on.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Central Market in Paramaribo open on Sunday?

No. The Centrale Markt on the Waterkant is open Monday to Saturday, from early morning until early afternoon. On Sunday go to a neighbourhood market such as Kwatta, Saoenah or the Tourtonnelaan instead.

What days is the Kwatta market open?

The Landbouwmarkt Kwatta on the Kwattaweg is held on Wednesday and Sunday mornings, and Sunday is the busiest day. Some sources also list a Friday market.

When is the Saoenah market?

Saoenah Markt in Paramaribo-Noord, on the corner of Anamoestraat and Jozef Israëlstraat, is a Sunday morning market. Come around 8 or 9 o'clock: popular Javanese dishes sell out before noon.

Where is the Maroon market in Paramaribo?

The Vreedzaam Market, often called the Maroon market, is in the old harbour shed right next to the Centrale Markt on the Waterkant. It sells medicinal plants, herbs and items for traditional healing, Monday to Saturday.

What time do markets in Suriname open?

Most markets open around dawn and are busiest before 10 in the morning. The Centrale Markt winds down in the early afternoon, and Sunday markets are mostly over by midday.
