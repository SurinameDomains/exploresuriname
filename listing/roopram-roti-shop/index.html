Roopram Roti Shop

Paramaribo, Suriname

Roopram Roti Shop is one of Paramaribo's most recognised roti restaurants, known for perfectly layered roti served with traditional Surinamese-Hindustani curries. A must-try for anyone exploring the city's food scene. The queues at lunch tell you everything you need to know.
