    About Heiligenweg Jam

    Heiligenweg is where Paramaribo's lijnbussen gather: buses with names painted on the side, music playing inside, and a line of people who all need to be somewhere else. In this game the lot is jammed solid. Every bus points one way and can only drive out if nothing stands in front of it, and only a handful of haltes are free at a time.

    Each passenger is going home: to Lelydorp, Nickerie, Albina, Commewijne, Brokopondo, Coronie, Saramacca or Wanica. Send out the right bus at the right time and the line melts away. Send out the wrong one and the haltes fill up with buses nobody in front of the line wants. Later chapters add taxis with two seats and mystery buses that only show their route once their road is clear.

    The levels never run out. When you need a break from the jam, try Korjaal Run, Aboma, Muskieto Survivor or the Sabi Suriname quiz.

    Common questions

    How do I play Heiligenweg Jam?

    Tap a lijnbus in the jammed lot. If the road in front of it is clear it drives out to a free halte at the top, otherwise it bumps into the bus in its way. Passengers wait in one line and board the bus going to their destination, and a full bus leaves. If every halte is taken and the first passenger in line cannot board any of the waiting buses, you are stuck, so send out the buses in the right order.

    How many levels are there?

    As many as you can play. Every level is built from its own number, so level 37 is the same puzzle for everyone, and every one of them can be solved. Levels come in chapters of 20 named after stops on the road out of the city, from Heiligenweg and the Waterkant to Lelydorp, Nickerie, Albina and Atjoni. Every fifth level is a tougher spits level.

    How do stars work?

    Clear a level without a single bump and you get three stars. One or two bumps cost you a star, more than that leaves you with one. Each level also gives you one free extra halte for when things get tight.

    What is the daily ride?

    One extra, tougher level that changes every day and is the same for every player. It does not affect your level progress, so you can always try it.

    Is it free?

    Yes. Heiligenweg Jam is free, needs no app or account, and your progress is saved in your browser.
