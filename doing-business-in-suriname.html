Frequently Asked Questions

Where did the business tools on Explore Suriname go?

They moved to SurinameBusiness.com, our sister site for Surinamese businesses. Old links forward to the same tool there automatically.

How do I start a business in Suriname?

Register the business with the Chamber of Commerce and Industry (KKF), get a tax number from the Belastingdienst and, once your turnover passes the threshold, register for BTW. SurinameBusiness.com has a step-by-step guide with the documents you need.

Does Explore Suriname still list businesses?

Yes. Shops, restaurants, hotels, services and tour operators stay listed on Explore Suriname, and adding yours is free.
