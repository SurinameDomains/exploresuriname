FOSI: Flashback of Sound

Saturday 5 December 2026 · in 57 days

Mingle Sushi, International Mall of Suriname, Ringweg-Zuid 245, Paramaribo

Add to calendarDirectionsSend on WhatsAppMingle Sushi →

Mingle Sushi rewinds three decades for one Saturday night: 70s, 80s and 90s classics back to back.

Flashback of Sound is a retro night built around the records people already know the words to, from disco and funk through to the dance floor fillers of the 90s. It runs at Mingle Sushi inside the International Mall of Suriname.

Good to know: Reserve a table on +597 727-4441. Saturday nights at the mall fill up early, so book ahead rather than walking in.
